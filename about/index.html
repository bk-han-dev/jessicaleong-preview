What Jessica does, and does not, do

Jessica acts for buyers as a Registered Estate Agent under PropNex Realty, and every transaction goes through PropNex. She is not a developer, a lawyer or a banker: she works with them on your behalf, and the developer's approved advertisement, the auction conditions and your Sale & Purchase Agreement are what bind the deal.

For project details, see KL Selangor Estates, where she keeps sourced, dated pages for projects in KLCC, Kuala Lumpur, Petaling Jaya, Selangor and Selangor's industrial parks, plus the Insights library.
