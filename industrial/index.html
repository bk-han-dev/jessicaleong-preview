Industrial property

Factories, warehouses and industrial property in Selangor

Jessica works with business owners and investors buying or renting semi-detached and detached factories, warehouses and units in industrial parks across Selangor. Enquire about pricing and availability.

What Jessica checks on an industrial purchase

Fit for the business

Built-up and land area, ceiling height, floor loading, power supply and access for your lorries.

Title and use

Tenure, land use category and any conditions on the title, checked with your lawyer.

Total cost

Price per sq ft of built-up and land, loan margin, stamp duty and fit-out, laid out before you commit.
