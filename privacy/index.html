Privacy Notice / Notis Privasi

Last updated 11 Nov 2026 · Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA)

Privacy Notice (English)

Who we are. This notice is issued by Jessica Leong, Registered Estate Agent E3208, practising with PropNex Realty Sdn Bhd (E (1) 1800/1) ("we"), for the website jessicaleongrealtor.com.

What we collect. When you use the enquiry form, WhatsApp, email or phone, we collect your name, mobile number, optional email address, the type of property you are interested in, your message, the page you sent it from, and the date and time.

Why we use it. To reply to your enquiry, including sending floor plans, availability, auction details and other information you request; to arrange viewings; and, with your agreement, to process a purchase. If you tick the optional box, we also send you updates on new launches in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor.

Source. We collect personal data directly from you.

Who we may share it with. PropNex Realty Sdn Bhd; and, only where needed for your enquiry or purchase and with your agreement, the relevant developer, auctioneer, panel lawyers and bankers. We do not sell your personal data.

Is it compulsory?. Your name, mobile number and consent are required so we can reply. If you do not provide them, we cannot respond through the form, but you can still WhatsApp or call us. Email, message and marketing updates are optional.

Your choices and rights. You may ask to access or correct your personal data, limit how we use it, or withdraw consent (including for marketing updates) at any time by contacting Jessica Leong on +6010-464 6088 or leongjes@gmail.com.

Security and retention. Enquiries are sent by email and may be kept in a private file on our web host that is not publicly accessible. We keep enquiries only as long as needed for the purposes above.

Website analytics. When measurement is switched on, this website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used, including which pages are viewed and whether a visitor clicks WhatsApp, a phone number or the enquiry form. IP addresses are anonymised. Google LLC may process this measurement data outside Malaysia, as described in Google's privacy policy. Measurement is not loaded if the browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Language. If there is any inconsistency between the English and Bahasa Malaysia versions, the English version prevails.

Notis Privasi (Bahasa Malaysia)

Siapa kami. Notis ini dikeluarkan oleh Jessica Leong, Ejen Harta Tanah Berdaftar E3208, yang beramal dengan PropNex Realty Sdn Bhd (E (1) 1800/1) ("kami"), bagi laman web jessicaleongrealtor.com.

Data yang dikumpul. Apabila anda menggunakan borang pertanyaan, WhatsApp, e-mel atau telefon, kami mengumpul nama, nombor telefon bimbit, alamat e-mel (pilihan), jenis hartanah yang diminati, mesej anda, halaman yang digunakan, serta tarikh dan masa.

Tujuan. Untuk menjawab pertanyaan anda; menghantar senarai harga, pelan lantai, butiran lelongan dan maklumat lain yang diminta; mengatur lawatan; dan, dengan persetujuan anda, memproses pembelian. Jika anda menandakan kotak pilihan, kami juga akan menghantar maklumat terkini tentang projek baharu di Kuala Lumpur dan Selangor.

Sumber. Data peribadi dikumpul terus daripada anda.

Pihak ketiga. PropNex Realty Sdn Bhd; dan, hanya jika perlu bagi pertanyaan atau pembelian anda serta dengan persetujuan anda, pemaju, pelelong, peguam panel dan pihak bank yang berkaitan. Kami tidak menjual data peribadi anda.

Wajib atau sukarela. Nama, nombor telefon bimbit dan persetujuan anda adalah wajib supaya kami dapat menjawab. Jika tidak diberikan, kami tidak dapat menjawab melalui borang, tetapi anda masih boleh menghubungi kami melalui WhatsApp atau telefon. E-mel, mesej dan maklumat pemasaran adalah pilihan.

Pilihan dan hak anda. Anda boleh meminta akses kepada atau pembetulan data peribadi anda, mengehadkan pemprosesannya, atau menarik balik persetujuan (termasuk bagi maklumat pemasaran) pada bila-bila masa dengan menghubungi Jessica Leong di +6010-464 6088 atau leongjes@gmail.com.

Analitis laman web. Apabila pengukuran diaktifkan, laman web ini menggunakan Google Analytics 4 untuk memahami cara laman ini digunakan, termasuk halaman yang dilawati dan sama ada pelawat mengklik WhatsApp, nombor telefon atau borang pertanyaan.
