Questions

Where can I see current prices?

Each project page on KL Selangor Estates lists the price range from the developer's approved advertisement with its date. Prices change, so please enquire with Jessica on WhatsApp about the project you are interested in.

What is the difference between KLCC and Cochrane new launches?

KLCC projects are mostly freehold or leasehold serviced residences in the city centre, usually at higher prices per square foot. Cochrane and Maluri, around MyTOWN and the Maluri MRT interchange, is a different market from the city centre, and prices, sizes and tenure vary from project to project. Jessica can put specific projects side by side.

What do I pay at booking?

Usually a booking fee set by the developer, then the balance of the 10% down payment when you sign the SPA. The amounts differ by project, so check the current terms with Jessica.
