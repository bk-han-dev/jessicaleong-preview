Common questions about working with Jessica

Is Jessica Leong a registered estate agent?

Yes. Jessica Leong is a Registered Estate Agent, registration number E3208, practising with PropNex Realty Sdn Bhd (E (1) 1800/1) at the KLCC branch in Marc Residence, Jalan Pinang. You can check both numbers on the LPEPH register at lpeph.gov.my/search-listing.

Which areas does Jessica cover?

KLCC and the Kuala Lumpur city centre, Cochrane and Maluri, Petaling Jaya and Damansara, and the wider Klang Valley in Selangor, including the industrial areas of Klang, Puchong, Kajang, Semenyih and Sepang.

What kinds of property does Jessica help with?

Three things: new-launch condominiums and serviced residences in KL and Selangor, industrial property such as factories and warehouses in Selangor, and bank lelong (auction) properties.

How is a bank lelong purchase different from a normal sale?

An auction property is sold under a proclamation of sale with set conditions: you usually pay a deposit (commonly 5% to 10% of the reserve price) on the day and settle the balance within a fixed period, and you buy the property as it is. Jessica helps you read the conditions and plan the numbers before you bid.

How quickly will Jessica reply?

WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach her. Tell her which project or area you are looking at and she will reply with the facts she has, or say when she will have them.
